# S4 Aaliyah, grade 4 student: review of School Atlas 5.0.0-beta.43

I am 9. I read slowly. This is my first map app. I use a school Chromebook or tablet and my teacher is close by. Sizes below are measured on the 1366-pixel-wide screenshots; on a touch screen my finger needs a target of roughly 44 pixels or more with a gap around it.

## Findings (most serious first)

**1. A "Guided Lesson" opens with no guiding on the screen.**
- Place: `06-map-opened.png`, whole screen; title bar reads "Exploring earthquakes and their causes (Guided Lesson)".
- What is there: the map, coloured dots, white lines, and a small black "i" circle (about 18 pixels) beside the title. No question, no first step, no "you are done" sign. The card in Open map did have a good question ("What causes earthquakes, and how do they affect people?") but it is gone once the map opens.
- Why it matters to me: I do not know what I am meant to find or when I am finished. I would not think to tap the tiny "i".
- Seriousness: stops the lesson (unless my teacher tells me every step).
- Change: show the map's question or first step on screen when the map opens, in one short sentence, with a big labelled button such as "About this map" in place of the bare "i". What the "i" shows is not in the packet: needs checking in the live app.

**2. The Legend panel is empty, so I cannot tell what the dots mean.**
- Place: `12-tool-legend.png`, top-right panel.
- What is there: the heading "Legend" and a close cross, nothing else, while yellow, red, blue and grey dots and white lines are on the map. The notes list only "Legend | ×" for this panel.
- Why it matters: the colours are the whole lesson. Without a key I am guessing.
- Seriousness: stops the lesson. It may only have been slow to fill in: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: make sure the key shows for every layer that is turned on, and say "Loading…" or "Nothing to show yet" instead of a blank box.

**3. The layer buttons are tiny pictures with no words, and one of them is a bin.**
- Place: `08-tool-layers.png` and `09-layer-menu.png`, the rows under each layer name.
- What is there: up to eight small icons per layer, each drawn about 16 to 18 pixels, centres about 44 pixels apart, none with a word. The last is a trash can. A second trash can sits in the panel's top bar about 44 pixels from the close cross. The on/off tick box is about 18 pixels and sits about 30 pixels from a small arrow.
- Why it matters: I cannot read a picture I have never seen, and my finger can land on the bin when I wanted the table or the cross. I just want to turn a layer on or off.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down; could stop it if a layer is deleted. Whether the bin asks "Are you sure?" or can be undone, and how big the real touch area is, are not in the packet: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: make the on/off box big and put it next to the layer name; keep the extra icons folded away by default (as the first layer is in `09-layer-menu.png`); add words to the icons; move both bins away from the close cross and ask before deleting.

**4. Opening Elevation or a table makes the bottom toolbar vanish.**
- Place: `17-tool-profile.png`, `18-profile-drawn.png`, `11-layer-table.png`.
- What is there: the row of big buttons (Basemaps … Save) is gone. A small square with nine dots (about 32 pixels) appears at the left under the scale bar, with no word on it.
- Why it matters: the big buttons were the one thing I had learned. I would think I broke it and put my hand up.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down. I assume the nine-dot square brings the tools back, but the packet does not show it being pressed: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: keep the toolbar in view, or label the nine-dot button "Tools" and make it as big as a toolbar button.

**5. The pop-up is full of words I cannot read.**
- Place: `07-map-popup.png` and `08-tool-layers.png`, white box in the middle.
- What is there: "2 Magnitude Earthquake", "1 km depth", "12 km SE of Livermore, CA, USA (38, -122)", "USGS PAGER ALERT:", "No PAGER Issued", and "1 of 17" with two very small arrows (about 10 pixels).
- Why it matters: "Magnitude", "PAGER ALERT" and "SE" mean nothing to me. "1 of 17" does not tell me I tapped 17 things at once, and the arrows are too small to tap.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down. The words come from the map, not the app, but they are what I see.
- Change: for lesson maps, use plain pop-up words ("Strength: 2", "How deep: 1 km", "Near: Livermore, California"); make the next/back arrows large and say "Earthquake 1 of 17 here".

**6. Finding a map means reading a long list or spelling a hard word.**
- Place: `04-tool-open.png` and `05-open-search.png`.
- What is there: "121 maps" over 13 pages, starting with "1775-1776 Revolutionary Battles". The instruction is "Select a map to open and explore it in the map view." in small grey letters. Category names include "Sustainable Development Goals", "Life and Environmental Science", "Civics and Government". There is a "Categories" button and a "Categories" heading. "Share" sits about 20 pixels from "Open map".
- Why it matters: I would have to spell "earthquake" myself or read many pages. The descriptions are long ("…shaped the first phase (1775-1776) of the American Revolution and why location mattered in the fight for independence."). I could tap "Share" by mistake.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down. Whether search forgives a spelling slip is not in the packet: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: let the teacher's link open the right map directly (the facts say a map can be shared as a link); shorten the instruction to "Pick a map."; move "Share" away from "Open map" or into the description fold.

**7. Nothing on the first screen says where to start.**
- Place: `01-first-open.png`.
- What is there: a world map and ten tool buttons. No welcome, no "start here".
- Why it matters: I am new. I would poke the map or wait for the teacher.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down.
- Change: one short line on first open, such as "Tap Open map to begin", pointing at that button.

**8. Tool names and tool instructions use hard words.**
- Places and exact words:
  - Toolbar (`01-first-open.png`): "Basemaps", "Map layers", "Legend", "Elevation", "Bookmarks".
  - `03-tool-basemaps.png`: "Choose the map underneath your layers." and names such as "Topographic (with Contours and Hillshade)", "Imagery (no labels)", "Charted Territory", "Enhanced Contrast".
  - `08-tool-layers.png`: "Turn layers on and off. Bookmarks remember these settings." (small grey text); the notes also give "Drag the handle, or press the up and down arrow keys on it, to change the drawing order."
  - `16-tool-measure.png`: "Coordinates", "Units".
  - `18-profile-drawn.png`: "Elevation profile", "Vertical exaggeration ×6.4", "Total climb and descent for lines up to 31 mi", and a line of source names ("Vantor, Airbus DS, USGS, NGA, NASA, CGIAR, GEBCO…").
  - `10-layer-filter.png`: "Show features where", "is at least", "Histogram", "Showing all 949 features".
  - `11-layer-table.png`: "(Total: 949 | Selection: 0)".
- Why it matters: I can sound out some of these but I do not know what they mean, so I cannot work alone.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down.
- Change: friendlier labels where the app owns the words ("Background map", "Map key", "How high?", "Show only…"); hide the source list and "Vertical exaggeration" behind a "More" button. The notes hold a much better sentence for the profile ("The line is 120 mi long. Its highest point is 10,607 ft and its lowest 3,950 ft.") that I cannot see in the screenshot: show it on screen.

**9. "Double-click to finish" does not fit a tablet, and the sentences are long.**
- Place: `16-tool-measure.png` ("Choose a tool, then click on the map. Double-click to finish.") and `17-tool-profile.png` ("Click the map for each point of a line and double-click to finish, or click a line already on the map.").
- Why it matters: on a tablet I tap, I do not click. The second sentence is 21 words with two "or" choices.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down. Whether a double tap works on a touch screen is not in the packet: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: "Tap the map to start a line. Tap again to add points. Tap Done to finish." and add a big "Done" button so no double tap is needed.

**10. Saving asks me things I do not understand.**
- Place: `24-tool-save.png`, `25-save-pressed.png`, `26-sign-in.png`.
- What is there: five tiles ("Save map", "Save as…", "Screenshot", "Print", "Sign in"). The explaining sentences listed in the notes are not visible on the tiles in the screenshot. The name box shows "rthquakes_and_their_causes_Guided_Lesson" (start cut off) and ".amap", then "This browser saves files to its Downloads folder. To choose a folder, use Chrome or Edge." Sign in says "Use your ArcGIS account to sign in."
- Why it matters: I do not know "Save map" from "Save as…", what ".amap" or a "Downloads folder" is, and I cannot change browser. I have no "ArcGIS account". The tiles themselves are a good big size.
- Seriousness: slows the lesson down (my teacher would do this part).
- Change: put "Save map" first and alone, with "Save as…" and "Sign in" under "More"; say "Your map will be saved on this computer." and drop the browser advice for students.

**11. Small buttons round the edges.**
- Place: every screenshot. Search, home, plus and minus at top left are about 32 pixels each, and plus and minus touch with no gap. Panel close crosses are drawn about 10 pixels. "2D" and "3D" are each about half the height of the other toolbar buttons (about 44 pixels) and stacked with no gap. The version tag "v5.0.0-beta.43" sits about 40 pixels right of "Save" and opens a long technical panel (`02-version-about.png`). The bookmark pencil "✎" (`21-tool-bookmarks.png`) is very small.
- Why it matters: I will hit minus when I want plus, 3D when I want 2D, and the version tag when I want Save.
- Seriousness: minor to slows it down. Real touch areas may be bigger than the drawn icons: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: make the edge buttons and close crosses at least 44 pixels with gaps; move the version tag away from Save.

**12. My own dots are hard to see, and one spelling is not the one I am taught.**
- Place: `14-csv-dropped.png` and `15-csv-added.png`.
- What is there: after "Add to map" the points are a few tiny dark specks on the satellite picture (they show as big coloured circles later, in `17-tool-profile.png`). The panel says "Found 4 points · 1 number columns, 1 category columns.", "Swap latitude and longitude", "Colour points by", "Colour & size".
- Why it matters: I would think nothing happened. "latitude and longitude" is too hard, "1 number columns" reads wrong, and my class spells it "color".
- Seriousness: minor for me (a teacher would likely do this step). The tiny dots may be the slow test machine: needs checking in the live app.
- Change: "color" in the US version, "1 number column", and zoom to the new points once they are drawn.

## What works well for me
- The ten bottom toolbar buttons are big (about 85 by 88 pixels), each has a picture and a word, and the one in use turns blue.
- It opens straight to a map with no sign-in, and "You don't need an account to use the app or to save maps on this device." is clear.
- Map cards have a picture, and "Open map" is an obvious outlined button; typing "earthquake" cut 121 maps to 11.
- "Looks good. Here's how we'll map it." and "+ Add bookmark" are friendly, short and easy to read.

## Would I use it?
With my teacher beside me I could open a map and look around, but I could not do a lesson on my own as it stands; the one change that would matter most is putting the lesson's question and first step on the screen when a map opens, with a working map key beside it.
