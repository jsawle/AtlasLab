# T2 Dana (first-year teacher): review of School Atlas 5.0.0-beta.43

Reviewed from the packet only (notes.txt, facts.txt, screenshots 01 to 27). I am strong on history content, new to running a class with technology, and my worries are: the map not loading, students getting lost, and not knowing what to say when the screen looks different from what I expected.

## Findings (most serious first)

### 1. There is nowhere to turn for help, in the app or outside it
- **Place:** every screenshot (no Help button anywhere on the toolbar or header); facts.txt.
- **What is there:** facts.txt says "no teacher how-to guide and no help link inside the app". The only explanatory text is the Versions panel (02-version-about.png), which is written for the people who built it: "White-label build of the MapMaker app: every MapMaker tool, local save, CSV data, ArcGIS Online sign-in."
- **Why it matters to me:** I need exact front-of-class steps to follow live. With ten tools and no guide, I would be working it out in front of 30 students.
- **Seriousness:** stops the lesson (I would not start one without a guide).
- **Suggested change:** a Help button on the toolbar that opens a one-page "first five minutes" guide: open a map, turn a layer on, click a point, get back to the start, save. Printable, so I can hold it.

### 2. A map called "Guided Lesson" opens with no guidance on screen
- **Place:** 06-map-opened.png, whole screen; title bar "Exploring earthquakes and their causes (Guided Lesson)" with a small black "i" button beside it.
- **What is there:** the map opens showing dots and white lines, with no steps, no question, no "start here". The card in Open map promised "What causes earthquakes, and how do they affect people?" (05-open-search.png) but that question is gone once the map is open.
- **Why it matters to me:** the word "Guided" tells me the app will lead the class. Nothing does. Students would ask "what do we do now?" and I would have no answer.
- **Seriousness:** stops the lesson.
- **Suggested change:** when a lesson map opens, show its question and first step in a panel straight away. Needs checking in the live app: the "i" button was not pressed in the walk-through, so I cannot tell whether the steps are behind it. If they are, the button needs a word on it ("Lesson steps" or "About this map").

### 3. No warning seen before unsaved work is replaced by another map
- **Place:** 27-open-another-unsaved.png, "Open map" window, second card ("Major Earthquakes (1950-2023)") with its "Open map" button pressed.
- **What is there:** the step is described as "With unsaved changes, another map chosen in Open map", but the picture and the on-screen words show no "you have unsaved changes" message.
- **Why it matters to me:** a student who clicks the wrong card would lose the period's work and I would be the one explaining why.
- **Seriousness:** stops the lesson for that student, if no warning exists. Needs checking in the live app: the screenshot may have been taken before a warning appeared.
- **Suggested change:** a plain message before the map changes: "Your map has changes that are not saved. Save first / Open without saving / Cancel".

### 4. The main toolbar vanishes when the table or the Elevation chart opens
- **Place:** 11-layer-table.png and 17-tool-profile.png to 19-profile-hover.png, bottom of the map.
- **What is there:** the row of ten tools (Basemaps ... Save) is gone. The compass and scale bar jump to a new place, and a small unlabelled square of dots appears at the left under the scale bar. In 11 the pop-up is also pushed half off the top of the map.
- **Why it matters to me:** this is exactly "the screen looks different from what I expected". I would not know how to get Save or Map layers back, and neither would students.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down (stops it if I do not find the way back).
- **Suggested change:** keep the toolbar in view above the table or chart. If it must hide, put the word "Tools" on the dots button. Needs checking in the live app: I am assuming the dots button brings the tools back; the packet does not show it pressed.

### 5. Layer buttons are pictures only, and delete sits next to close
- **Place:** 08-tool-layers.png, "Map layers" panel on the right.
- **What is there:** each layer has a row of up to seven small picture buttons with no words (magnifier, list, squares, table, funnel, "i", bin). At the top of the panel a bin sits right beside the "×" close button. The only instruction is "Turn layers on and off. Bookmarks remember these settings."
- **Why it matters to me:** I cannot say "click the funnel-looking one" with confidence to a class. A slip from close to the bin at the top could, I assume, remove layers in the middle of a lesson.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down. Needs checking in the live app: whether the buttons show a name when pointed at, what the top bin removes, and whether it asks first.
- **Suggested change:** show a word under or beside each button (Zoom to, Legend, Table, Filter, About, Remove), move the top bin away from the close button, and make it ask before removing anything.

### 6. Legend opens empty
- **Place:** 12-tool-legend.png, "Legend" panel top right.
- **What is there:** the panel has the heading "Legend" and nothing else, although the map has yellow, red, blue and grey dots and white lines.
- **Why it matters to me:** the first student question will be "what do the red ones mean?". The Legend is where I would look, and it tells me nothing, not even why it is blank.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down (stops it if the lesson depends on reading the colours). Needs checking in the live app: it may have been filling in when the picture was taken.
- **Suggested change:** if it is still loading, say "Loading legend…". If there is nothing to show, say why in words.

### 7. One click on the map gives "1 of 17" with a half-drawn pop-up
- **Place:** 07-map-popup.png, pop-up in the centre; compare 08-tool-layers.png.
- **What is there:** in 07 the pop-up has a blank band at the top with only "1 of 17", and no close button or arrows. In 08 the same pop-up has a close "×", "Zoom to", arrows and "1 of 17" at the bottom. The first result is "2 Magnitude Earthquake" with "USGS PAGER ALERT: No PAGER Issued".
- **Why it matters to me:** I clicked one place and got seventeen answers with no explanation of what "17" counts. Students on the same step would each see something different from me and from the sheet. "PAGER" is a word I could not explain if asked (that wording comes from the map, not the app).
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down.
- **Suggested change:** say what the count is ("17 things at this spot, showing 1") and make sure the pop-up never appears without its close button. Needs checking in the live app: whether the 07 look lasts more than a moment.

### 8. Grey layer names, a dot on a funnel, and a filter on a hidden layer: none explained
- **Place:** 08-tool-layers.png and 10-layer-filter.png.
- **What is there:** "Historic Major Earthquakes" and "Other causes of earthquakes" are grey and unticked; "Global Active Earthquake Faults" is also grey but ticked. One funnel button has a blue dot. In 10 the Filter panel for "Historic Major Earthquakes" (a layer that is switched off) says "Showing all 949 features" while none of them are on the map.
- **Why it matters to me:** "it is ticked but I cannot see it" is a mismatch I could not explain. I am guessing grey-and-ticked means "zoom in to see it" and the dot means "already filtered", but the screen does not say so. "Features" is not a word I or my students use.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down.
- **Suggested change:** put the reason in words beside the layer ("Zoom in to see this layer", "Filtered"), and in Filter say "This layer is switched off" when it is. Use "places" or "items" or explain "features" once.

### 9. Switching to 3D: nothing says it is working, and the layers are not in the picture
- **Place:** 22-to-3d.png, whole screen.
- **What is there:** 45 seconds after pressing 3D the screen shows a tilted, cloudy view of the Earth with none of the coloured points that were on the 2D map, and no "loading" message. The packet says the test machine had no graphics card, so it was slow there.
- **Why it matters to me:** my worry is the map not loading. With no message I cannot tell "slow" from "broken", and I would not know whether to wait or go back to 2D.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down. Needs checking on a real class Chromebook.
- **Suggested change:** show "Loading 3D view…" until the layers are drawn, and after a long wait offer "This is taking a while. Go back to 2D".

### 10. After "Add to map", nothing confirms my data was added
- **Place:** 14-csv-dropped.png and 15-csv-added.png.
- **What is there:** dropping the file moved the map from the earthquake view to a close-up of Utah without asking. After pressing "Add to map" the panel closes; the only sign anything happened is the number on Map layers changing from 5 to 6. No coloured points are visible in 15 (they are there later, in 17 and 20). Two style choices ("Colour & size", "Pie charts") are greyed out with no reason given.
- **Why it matters to me:** I would think it had failed and do it again, or tell the class it did not work.
- **Seriousness:** minor to slows it down. Needs checking in the live app: the points may simply have been drawing when the picture was taken.
- **Suggested change:** a short message after adding ("parks added: 4 points. See it in Map layers"), and a line saying why a style is greyed out.

### 11. Sketch tells me to use tools I can barely see
- **Place:** 20-tool-sketch.png, right side.
- **What is there:** the panel says "Choose a drawing tool on the right of the map, or select a sketch to change it." The tools are a thin white strip to the left of that panel, mostly empty, with four small faint pictures and no words.
- **Why it matters to me:** "on the right of the map" points me at the panel I am already reading. The strip looks unfinished, so I would assume something had not loaded.
- **Seriousness:** slows the lesson down. Needs checking in the live app: whether the strip normally shows more tools than this.
- **Suggested change:** put the drawing tools inside the Sketch panel with a word on each (Point, Line, Shape, Text), or have the message point at the strip.

### 12. Small things that would make me think something is wrong
- **06-map-opened.png:** white bands down both sides of the opened map look like a map that has not finished loading. A "5" on Map layers and a blue dot on Bookmarks appear with no explanation (Bookmarks in 21 lists only "Global view").
- **18-profile-drawn.png, bottom row:** "Total climb and descent for lines up to 31 mi" reads as a label with its number missing. My line was 120 mi; say "Total climb and descent: not shown for lines over 31 mi".
- **14-csv-dropped.png:** "1 number columns, 1 category columns" should read "1 number column, 1 category column".
- **Every screen, bottom right:** "v5.0.0-beta.43". facts.txt confirms it is a test version. I would want to know whether it will look the same next week.
- **Test run, notes.txt:** two steps could not be completed by the test (the information button on a map card, and pressing "Distance" in Measure). I cannot tell from the packet what a person would see at those points, or what the app says when a map fails to load: no error message appears anywhere in the packet. Needs checking in the live app, because "what do I do when it goes wrong" is my main question.
- **Seriousness:** minor each.

## What works well for me
- It opens straight to a map with no account or sign-in, and the sign-in window says so plainly: "You don't need an account to use the app or to save maps on this device."
- The main toolbar has a word under every picture (Basemaps, Open map, Map layers, Legend, Measure, Save), so I can call out a step by name.
- Search in Open map is quick to follow and tells me what happened: "11 of 121 maps match".
- The Elevation chart sums itself up in a sentence I could read aloud: "The line is 120 mi long. Its highest point is 10,607 ft and its lowest 3,950 ft."

## Would I use it?
Not in front of a class as it stands, though I would try it on my own first; the single change that would matter most is a Help button with a short printed-style guide of exact steps, including what to do when the screen does not look as expected.
