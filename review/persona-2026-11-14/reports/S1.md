# S1 Jordan (13, 8th grade, reads below grade level) - review of School Atlas 5.0.0-beta.43

Judged only from the packet (notes.txt, facts.txt, screenshots 01 to 27). Jordan is quick with a Chromebook and will click things to see what they do. What stops him is long sentences, hard words, and screens that do not say what to do next.

## Findings (most serious first)

### 1. A "Guided Lesson" opens with no steps on screen
- **Place:** 06-map-opened.png, whole screen and the title bar.
- **What is there:** The title reads "Exploring earthquakes and their causes (Guided Lesson)". The map shows dots and white lines. Nothing says what to do first. The only clue is a small black "i" circle next to the title, with no word on it. The card in Open map asked "What causes earthquakes, and how do they affect people?" but that question is gone once the map opens.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** The name promises a guide. He sees none. He will not guess that a small "i" holds the steps (if it does). He does not know what to look at, what to write, how much, or where. He will put his hand up or start clicking at random.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** The "i" button was never opened in the walk, so I do not know if it holds steps, how long they are, or whether there is anywhere to write an answer. Needs checking in the live app. For the same reason I cannot judge "can I finish in the time".
- **Suggested change:** When a lesson map opens, show the first step straight away in a short panel (one step at a time, one short sentence each, with a "Next" button). Put a word on the "i" button, such as "Steps" or "About this map".

### 2. The Legend panel is empty
- **Place:** 12-tool-legend.png, top right panel.
- **What is there:** The panel shows only the word "Legend" and a close button. The notes confirm the panel held nothing else. The map behind it has yellow, red, light blue and grey dots and white lines.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** This is the one place that should tell him what the colours mean. An empty box looks broken. He cannot answer any question about the dots without it.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson if it stays empty; minor if it is only slow to fill.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** Whether it fills in after a wait (the test machine was slow). Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** If it is loading, say "Loading..." in the panel. If no layer has a legend, say so in plain words. Consider a friendlier label: "Legend" could have a second line, "What the colours mean".

### 3. Map layers: rows of small icons with no words
- **Place:** 08-tool-layers.png and 09-layer-menu.png, right panel.
- **What is there:** Each layer has a row of up to seven small picture buttons (magnifier, list, squares, table, funnel, "i", bin) with no labels. Layers that are switched off have grey names. A second bin sits at the top of the panel next to the close button. The help line reads "Turn layers on and off. Bookmarks remember these settings." The notes also list this sentence in the panel: "Drag the handle, or press the up and down arrow keys on it, to change the drawing order." (I cannot see it in the screenshot.)
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** He cannot tell the buttons apart without trying each one. Two of them are bins. One wrong click could remove a layer, or (the top bin) maybe all of them. "Bookmarks remember these settings." means nothing to him here: he has not used Bookmarks yet. "drawing order" is a phrase he will not know.
- **How serious:** Slows it down; could stop the lesson if he deletes a layer by mistake.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** Whether the icons show a name when the pointer is over them, what the top bin removes, and whether there is an undo or an "Are you sure?" Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** Show a short word under or beside each icon ("Zoom", "Key", "Table", "Filter", "About", "Remove"). Ask before removing. Cut the help line to "Tick a layer to show it." Replace "drawing order" with "Drag to move a layer above or below another."

### 4. The first screen does not say where to start, and the button words are mapping words
- **Place:** 01-first-open.png, bottom toolbar.
- **What is there:** A world map and a row of buttons: "Basemaps", "Open map", "Map layers", "Add layer", "Legend", "Measure", "Elevation", "Sketch", "2D", "3D", "Bookmarks", "Save". No welcome line and no hint.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** "Open map", "Measure", "Sketch" and "Save" are fine. "Basemaps", "Map layers", "Add layer", "Legend" and "Elevation" are words he will not know. If the teacher says "open the earthquake map" he will find "Open map". If not, he has no idea which button comes first.
- **How serious:** Slows it down.
- **Suggested change:** One short line on first open, for example "Start here: Open map" pointing at that button. Keep the labels but add a few plain words when the pointer rests on a button, for example Basemaps: "Change the background map"; Legend: "What the colours mean"; Elevation: "How high the land is".

### 5. Filter uses words he will not know
- **Place:** 10-layer-filter.png, right panel.
- **What is there:** "Show features where", a box set to "Depth", a box set to "is at least", "Enter a number", "Histogram" with a bar chart from "-0.1" to "675.4", "Add new", "Showing all 949 features", "Apply", "Cancel", "Clear all".
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** "features" and "Histogram" will stop him. The chart has no units and no label, so "-0.1" to "675.4" means nothing. "Add new" does not say add new what. He will not know what number to type.
- **How serious:** Slows it down (stops the lesson if a step needs a filter).
- **Suggested change:** "Show only the earthquakes where" (or "Show only the places where"). Hide the histogram behind a plain label such as "See how the numbers are spread", and show the units. "Add new" could be "Add another rule". "Showing all 949 features" could be "Showing all 949".

### 6. Adding your own spreadsheet: a long panel with too many choices
- **Place:** 14-csv-dropped.png, right panel; 15-csv-added.png for what happens next.
- **What is there:** The heading is friendly: "Looks good. Here's how we'll map it." Then: "Found 4 points · 1 number columns, 1 category columns.", a button "Swap latitude and longitude", "MAP STYLE" with six tiles ("Locations", "By type", "By size", "Colour & size", "Type & size", "Pie charts"), the hint "Type & size — e.g. species as colour, height as size", then "COLUMNS" with "Colour points by", "Size points by", "Label points with", then "POINTS" with shape and size. The panel runs past the bottom of the screen. Two tiles are greyed out with no reason given.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** The good news is he can just press "Add to map" and it works. But the panel looks like a form he has to fill in, and that is where he gives up. "Swap latitude and longitude", "category columns", "e.g." and "species" are stoppers. "1 number columns" reads as a mistake. "Colour" is the British spelling; a US student will read it as a typo.
- **How serious:** Slows it down.
- **Also:** In 15-csv-added.png, right after "Add to map", I cannot see the new points on the map. In later shots (17, 23) they show as big coloured circles. Jordan may think nothing happened. May only be slow drawing on the test machine. Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** Show the heading, one line ("We found 4 places.") and "Add to map" first. Put everything else under one "Change how it looks" button. Fix "1 number columns" to "1 number column". Say why a tile is grey. Show a short "Added 4 points" message after adding.

### 7. Elevation: one long instruction, hard words, and the toolbar goes away
- **Place:** 17-tool-profile.png, 18-profile-drawn.png, 19-profile-hover.png, bottom panel.
- **What is there:** The instruction is one sentence of 22 words: "Click the map for each point of a line and double-click to finish, or click a line already on the map." The header carries a long credit line: "Elevation data. Sources: Vantor, Airbus DS, USGS, NGA, NASA, CGIAR, GEBCO, N Robinson, NCEAS, NLS, OS, NMA, Geodatastyrelsen and the GIS User Community". Under the chart: "Length 120 mi", "Highest 10,607 ft", "Lowest 3,950 ft", "Vertical exaggeration ×6.4", "Total climb and descent for lines up to 31 mi". Buttons: "Undo point", "New profile". While this panel is open the bottom toolbar is gone and a small button with nine dots appears on the left with no label (also in 11-layer-table.png).
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** "Length", "Highest", "Lowest" are clear and the chart itself is easy to read. "Elevation profile", "Vertical exaggeration ×6.4" and "Total climb and descent for lines up to 31 mi" are not. The last one reads like half a sentence: he cannot tell it means his line is too long. The credit line is a wall of capital letters next to the title. With the toolbar gone, he may not know how to get his tools back.
- **How serious:** Slows it down.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** What the nine-dot button does. Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** Split the instruction: "1. Click on the map to start a line. 2. Click again for each bend. 3. Double-click to finish." Change "Vertical exaggeration ×6.4" to "Hills drawn 6.4 times taller" (the notes show the app already has a plain sentence like this: "Heights are drawn 6.4 times as tall as distances."). Change the last item to "Line too long to add up the climb (limit 31 mi)". Move the credits behind a small "Sources" link. Label the nine-dot button "Tools".

### 8. Save: two buttons that sound the same, and a file type he has never seen
- **Place:** 24-tool-save.png, right panel; 25-save-pressed.png, middle window.
- **What is there:** Five tiles: "Save map", "Save as…", "Screenshot", "Print", "Sign in". In the screenshot the tiles show only these names. The notes list a sentence for each (for example "Save your map as an .amap file, wherever you choose. No sign-in needed. After the first save, Save updates the same file."), but I cannot see those sentences in the screenshot. The name window says "Save your map", a long file name ending "...rthquakes_and_their_causes_Guided_Lesson", ".amap", and "This browser saves files to its Downloads folder. To choose a folder, use Chrome or Edge."
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** Short tile names are good for him. But he cannot tell "Save map" from "Save as…". ".amap" is new, and nothing says how to get the map back later (the answer is "Open map", then "My map files", but the Save panel never says so). "To choose a folder, use Chrome or Edge." is a message for an adult.
- **How serious:** Slows it down; it becomes serious the next day if he cannot find his work.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** Where the longer tile sentences appear (on hover?), and what he sees after saving on a real Chromebook, where facts.txt says Chrome asks where to put the file. Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** Rename "Save as…" to "Save a copy". After saving, show one line: "Saved. To get it back: Open map, then My map files." Offer a shorter starting file name.

### 9. Opening another map with unsaved work: no warning visible
- **Place:** 27-open-another-unsaved.png, Open map window.
- **What is there:** The step is described as "With unsaved changes, another map chosen in Open map." The screenshot shows the Open map list with "Open map" pressed on "Major Earthquakes (1950-2023)". No message about unsaved work is on screen, and the notes list no such message.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** He clicks fast. If his sketch or added data vanishes without a warning, he will not redo it.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson if work is lost.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** Whether a warning appears a moment later, and what it says. Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** If there is no warning, add a short one: "You have not saved this map. Save it first?" with "Save", "Don't save", "Cancel".

### 10. Open map: easy to search, but a long list with hard descriptions
- **Place:** 04-tool-open.png and 05-open-search.png.
- **What is there:** "Select a map to open and explore it in the map view." Then "121 maps" over 13 pages, a "Categories" button and a "Categories" heading (the word appears twice), tabs "All 121", "2D maps 118", "3D maps 3", and on each card "Share", "Open map" and "Map's description". Card text includes "ACS Race and Hispanic Origin by County" (no description at all), "This layer contains particulate matter 2.5 (PM 2.5) air quality data globally between 1998 and 2016 based on NASA SEDAC gridded data.", and "Explore major (magnitude of 7.0 or higher) global earthquakes between 1950-2023 filtered from the United States Geological Survey (USGS) Earthquake Catalog."
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** If the teacher gives him a word to type, Search works well: "earthquake" gives "11 of 121 maps match" and the pictures help him pick. Without a search word, 13 pages is too many and he will not read the descriptions. Category names like "Sustainable Development Goals" and "Civics and Government" are hard. He may press "Share" by mistake as it sits right beside "Open map".
- **How serious:** Slows it down. (The descriptions come from the maps, not the app, so this is partly a content matter.)
- **Suggested change:** Keep the card text to the title and one short line; put the rest under "Map's description". Remove one of the two "Categories". Make "Open map" the solid coloured button so it stands out from "Share".

### 11. Sketch: the tip points to tools that look blank
- **Place:** 20-tool-sketch.png, top right.
- **What is there:** The tip reads "Choose a drawing tool on the right of the map, or select a sketch to change it." Beside the panel is a tall thin bar. In the screenshot only four small icons show on it (an arrow, a pin, an undo arrow, a cog) with large empty gaps between them. The notes also list a button "Go to the drawing tools", which I cannot see in the screenshot.
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** The tip is short enough. But the bar it points to looks half empty, with no words, so he cannot find "line" or "shape". The second half, "or select a sketch to change it", is extra reading he does not need yet.
- **How serious:** Slows it down.
- **Cannot tell from the packet:** Whether the gaps are tools that had not finished loading on the test machine. Needs checking in the live app.
- **Suggested change:** Tip: "Pick a drawing tool from the bar." Put names on the tools or show them on hover. Show the second tip only after he has drawn something.

### 12. "Sign in" and the version button invite clicks that lead to adult text
- **Place:** 26-sign-in.png (middle window); 02-version-about.png (right panel); the "Sign in" button top right on every screen; the "v5.0.0-beta.43" button bottom right.
- **What is there:** Sign in window: "Sign in with ArcGIS", "Use your ArcGIS account to sign in. Signing in lets you save maps to your ArcGIS Online account.", then "You don't need an account to use the app or to save maps on this device.", "Cancel", "Continue". Versions panel: "Version numbers for this app and each of its parts." and a table starting "White-label build of the MapMaker app: every MapMaker tool, local save, CSV data, ArcGIS Online sign-in."
- **Why it matters to Jordan:** A bold "Sign in" button at the top of every screen looks like something he must do. He has no ArcGIS account. The sentence that helps him ("You don't need an account...") comes second, and the highlighted button is "Continue". The Versions panel is not for him, but he will click the button and meet a wall of text.
- **How serious:** Minor (sign in could slow it down if he presses "Continue").
- **Suggested change:** Put "You don't need an account to use the app or to save maps on this device." first. Add a first line to the Versions panel: "This is for teachers and testers."

## What works well for Jordan
- Big toolbar buttons with a picture and one or two words each; nothing to install and no sign-in before the map appears.
- Search in Open map: one word typed, "11 of 121 maps match", and a picture on every card.
- Measure has the shortest and clearest instruction in the app: "Choose a tool, then click on the map. Double-click to finish."
- The elevation chart: hovering shows a plain reading ("59 mi along: 7,985 ft"), and "Length", "Highest", "Lowest" are words he knows.

## Would Jordan use it?
Jordan would happily click around the map and could open a map by search on his own, but he would stall as soon as a lesson needed him to follow steps, so the single change that matters most is showing the lesson's steps on screen, one short step at a time, as soon as a "Guided Lesson" opens.
