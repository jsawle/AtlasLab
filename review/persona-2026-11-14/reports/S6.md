# S6 Mina: low vision, browser zoomed to 300% or 400%

Reviewed from the packet only: notes.txt, facts.txt, screenshots 01 to 27, the six zoom screenshots (z300-*, z400-*) and the MAGNIFIED section of access.txt. Contrast figures are estimates measured from the screenshot pixels, so treat them as approximate. The Word sheet is not in the material I was given, so her checks on the sheet (large print, order when enlarged) are not covered here.

## Findings, most serious first

**1. At 400% there is almost no map left to look at (stops the lesson)**
- Place: z400-1-first-open.png, whole screen; z400-3-save.png.
- What is there: the header ("School Atlas", "Sign in") takes the top third, the tool bar takes another third, and the compass, the version button and a blank white box sit on what is left. Only a thin strip of map shows. With the Save panel open (z400-3) no useful map shows at all.
- Why it matters to Mina: 400% is her normal working zoom. She can reach the buttons but cannot see the thing the lesson is about.
- Suggested change: at small page sizes, shrink the header to one thin line (or hide it), drop the compass and version button, and let the tool bar collapse to a single "Tools" button so the map gets most of the screen.

**2. Open map at 400%: no maps can be seen in the window (stops the lesson)**
- Place: z400-2-open-map.png; z300-2-open-map.png; access.txt "the first card's Open map button on screen without scrolling = False".
- What is there: at 400% the window shows only "Open map", "Select a map to open and explore it in the map view." and the two tabs. The search box, the categories and every map card are below the bottom edge. At 300% the title, sentence, tabs and search fill the window and the list gets a sliver at the bottom.
- Why it matters: this is how she gets to the lesson map. access.txt says "the window scrolls = True", but the packet does not show whether the title and tabs scroll away with the list or stay fixed and leave the list a tiny slot. Needs checking in the live app at 400%.
- Suggested change: at small sizes make the whole window one scrolling page (title, tabs, search and cards scroll together), hide the one-sentence description, and let the window use the full screen.

**3. A link is drawn on top of the map filter buttons in Open map at 300% (slows it down)**
- Place: z300-2-open-map.png, bottom of the window.
- What is there: "Start a new blank map" is drawn straight over the row of "All / 2D maps / 3D maps" buttons, so both are unreadable and it is unclear which one a click would hit.
- Why it matters: she sees only this small part of the screen, and that part is garbled.
- Suggested change: at small sizes put "Start a new blank map" and the page numbers in the normal flow under the list instead of pinned to the bottom of the window.

**4. Save, Bookmarks and 2D/3D are off the screen at 300% and 400%; Elevation and Sketch too at 400% (slows it down, could stop saving)**
- Place: z300-1 and z400-1, tool bar; access.txt MAGNIFIED lines ("tool-save:off screen" at both zooms; "tool-profile:off screen, tool-sketch:off screen" at 400%).
- What is there: the tool bar runs off the right edge. A cut-off icon at the edge is the only hint that there is more. The page itself does not scroll sideways.
- Why it matters: Save is the last tool in the row, so the one she needs at the end of a lesson is the hardest to find. Whether the bar can be dragged or scrolled sideways cannot be told from the packet: needs checking in the live app.
- Suggested change: wrap the tool bar onto two rows at small sizes, or add a clear "More" button; keep Save always in view.

**5. The tool bar loses its words when zoomed (slows it down)**
- Place: compare 01-first-open.png (icons with "Basemaps", "Open map", "Map layers", "Add layer", "Legend", "Measure", "Elevation", "Sketch", "Bookmarks", "Save") with z300-1 and z400-1 (icons only).
- What is there: at 300% and 400% the labels are gone and only thin line drawings remain. "Open map" and "Map layers" look very alike.
- Why it matters: a worksheet step such as "click Map layers" gives her a word to look for, and the word is not there. Thin-line icons are also the hardest thing for her to tell apart.
- Suggested change: keep the word under each icon at every zoom, even if that means two rows. Whether a name appears on hover needs checking in the live app.

**6. Save panel at 300% and 400%: the tiles are cut off so their names cannot be read (slows it down)**
- Place: z300-3-save.png and z400-3-save.png.
- What is there: at 300% the panel shows two tile pictures but not the words "Save map" and "Save as…" under them, and none of the other three tiles (Screenshot, Print, Sign in). At 400% only the top edge of the first two tiles shows, and the panel covers part of the "School Atlas" header. access.txt counts the tiles as "on screen", but their labels are not.
- Why it matters: she has to guess which picture is Save. Whether the panel scrolls is not shown: needs checking in the live app.
- Suggested change: at small sizes show the Save choices as a plain list of words, one per line, in a panel that scrolls.

**7. Controls pile on top of each other on the zoomed map (slows it down)**
- Place: z300-1-first-open.png left side and middle; z400-1-first-open.png.
- What is there: at 300% the compass sits over the Home and zoom buttons, so only the search button shows. At 400% the compass covers the search button as well and half a "+" button pokes out below the tool bar. A blank white box also runs across the middle of the map at both zooms; the packet does not say what it is (needs checking in the live app).
- Why it matters: she cannot reach Home or the map's own zoom buttons, and the white box hides map she has little enough of.
- Suggested change: hide the compass at small sizes, keep search, Home, + and - in one row that never overlaps, and find and remove the white box.

**8. Earthquake lesson map: pale dots on pale land, white line on white ice (slows it down)**
- Place: 06-map-opened.png and 07-map-popup.png, the map itself.
- What is there: many of the earthquake points are small light-grey dots on light-grey and white land (western United States, Alaska). The white plate-boundary line disappears where it crosses Greenland and other white areas. The small turquoise dots are also tiny. Yellow and red dots on the dark ocean are clear.
- Why it matters: pale on pale is exactly what she cannot pick out, and the grey dots are most of the recent earthquakes over land.
- Suggested change: this is a matter for the map's own styling: give the grey dots a dark outline and the white line a dark edge, and make the smallest points bigger. Whether the app's "Enhanced Contrast" basemap helps on this map needs checking in the live app.

**9. Legend panel is empty, so colours and sizes are not explained (slows it down; needs checking)**
- Place: 12-tool-legend.png, top right. notes.txt records only "Legend | ×".
- What is there: the Legend panel opens with a title and nothing in it, on a map with yellow, red, turquoise and grey dots and white lines.
- Why it matters: when she cannot tell dots apart by look, the legend is her fallback. It may only have been slow to load on the test machine: needs checking in the live app, and at 300% for whether it then covers the map.
- Suggested change: make sure the legend fills, show "Loading…" while it does, and use large swatches with the words beside them.

**10. Pop-up text is small, italic and partly grey (minor at zoom; needs checking)**
- Place: 07-map-popup.png and 08-tool-layers.png, the pop-up.
- What is there: under the bold "2 Magnitude Earthquake" the facts ("1 km depth", "November 14, 2026", "No PAGER Issued") are in small italics, and the place, "12 km SE of Livermore, CA, USA (38, -122)", is a grey underlined link (about 5:1, so it passes, but it is the palest line and the key fact of where). The pop-up is about a third of the screen wide at 100%; no zoomed picture of it is in the packet, so whether it fits or covers everything at 300% needs checking in the live app.
- Why it matters: the depth, date and place are the answers a worksheet asks for.
- Suggested change: a matter for the map's pop-up settings: upright text, dark colour for the place, one fact per line.

**11. Greyed-out choices are too faint to read (minor)**
- Place: 14-csv-dropped.png, "Colour & size" and "Pie charts" tiles (about 2.2:1); 04-tool-open.png, the "previous page" arrow at the bottom (about 1.7:1); 08-tool-layers.png, layer names "Historic Major Earthquakes", "Other causes of earthquakes" and "Global Active Earthquake Faults" in grey (about 5.3:1, readable).
- What is there: unavailable choices are shown only by being pale. "Global Active Earthquake Faults" is grey even though its box is ticked, with no words saying why.
- Why it matters: she cannot read what the faint tiles say, so cannot tell what is unavailable or why; and grey-but-ticked looks the same to her as switched off.
- Suggested change: keep unavailable choices dark enough to read and add words ("not available for this data", "zoom in to see this layer").

**12. No way to make the app's own text bigger or switch to high contrast, and no help link (minor)**
- Place: facts.txt ("no accessibility statement ... no help link inside the app"); all screenshots.
- What is there: panel help text and hints are small grey text (for example "Turn layers on and off. Bookmarks remember these settings."). Nothing in the packet shows the app in a high-contrast mode: needs checking in the live app with the Chromebook's high-contrast setting.
- Why it matters: browser zoom is her only tool, and findings 1 to 7 show what it costs her.
- Suggested change: test and publish what works at 400% and in high-contrast mode, and link to it from the app.

## Works well for Mina
- No sideways scrolling of the page at 300% or 400% (access.txt), and "School Atlas" and "Sign in" stay large and sharp.
- The app's own text has strong contrast: tool labels, card titles, table and elevation figures are dark on white (about 9:1 to 13:1); even the grey help text is about 5:1.
- Map labels and the credits line get bigger with browser zoom instead of staying tiny (z300-1).
- The elevation result is also written out in words: "The line is 120 mi long. Its highest point is 10,607 ft and its lowest 3,950 ft.", so she need not read the chart.

## Verdict
Mina could not use it as it stands at her usual 400%, because the header, tool bar and panels leave almost no map and no map list to see; the single change that would matter most is a small-screen layout that gives the map and the open panel the whole screen, with the tool bar labelled and wrapping so that Save is always in view.
