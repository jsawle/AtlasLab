# S7 Devon: review of School Atlas 5.0.0-beta.43

I am 13, I use the keyboard and a trackball, and my hands are not steady. I checked: can I do each step with keys, can I get out, does anything need a drag, a double-click, a hover or a tiny target, and can I see where I am. Based on the keyboard run in access.txt, shots k1 to k3, and the walk-through shots 01 to 27.

## Findings (most serious first)

**1. Measure and Elevation cannot be done from the keyboard, and both end with a double-click.**
- Place: 16-tool-measure.png (Measure panel, top right) and 17-tool-profile.png / 18-profile-drawn.png (Elevation dock).
- What is there: Measure says "Choose a tool, then click on the map. Double-click to finish." Elevation says "Click the map for each point of a line and double-click to finish, or click a line already on the map." The keyboard run got only these messages: no way was offered to place points with keys, and the "Use a sketched line" list was not shown.
- Why it matters to me: I can't place points with keys at all, and a double-click on the same spot with a trackball is the hardest thing for my hands; the ball moves between clicks.
- Seriousness: stops the lesson, for any task that asks for a distance or a profile.
- Change: let Enter drop a point at a crosshair in the middle of the map (move the map with arrows, press Enter), and add a visible "Finish" button and Enter-to-finish so a double-click is never needed. Say so in the message.

**2. Opening a pop-up for a place on the map: no keyboard way is shown, and the dots are small.**
- Place: 07-map-popup.png (pop-up "2 Magnitude Earthquake", "1 of 17"); the dots on the map in 06 and 07.
- What is there: the pop-up came from a mouse click in the middle of the map. The earthquake dots are a few pixels across and sit close together (17 at that one click). The keyboard run did not test opening a pop-up, moving through "1 of 17" or closing it.
- Why it matters: reading a pop-up is the main thing a lesson asks. If it needs an exact click on a small dot, I will hit the wrong one or miss.
- Seriousness: stops the lesson if there is no keyboard way; needs checking in the live app.
- Change: a keyboard way to step through the features in view (or open the pop-up from a row of the layer's table, 11-layer-table.png), and a bigger click area around small dots.

**3. When focus is on the map, nothing shows it.**
- Place: access.txt, Tab press 4 (and 28): map "[NO VISIBLE FOCUS RING]"; k1-tab-focus.png shows how clear the ring is on the compass by comparison.
- What is there: the map takes focus and the keys work there ('+' zoomed 6 to 7, right arrow moved it), but I can't see that I'm on it. (07 and 18 show a thin blue line round the map after a mouse click; whether that should also appear on Tab needs checking.)
- Why it matters: the map is the one place where the keys do something different. I would press arrows and not know why nothing, or something, moved.
- Seriousness: slows it down, a lot.
- Change: a thick, clear ring round the map when it has focus, the same as the compass ring.

**4. The focus mark on the bottom tool bar is only a thin line on one side.**
- Place: k2-focus-on-open-map.png, bottom bar, "Open map" button.
- What is there: focus is on "Open map" but all I can see is a thin blue line down its left edge, next to "Basemaps". It could be read as either button.
- Why it matters: this bar is where every tool starts. One wrong Enter opens the wrong panel and costs me several more presses.
- Seriousness: slows it down.
- Change: draw the whole ring inside the button, as clear as on the card button in k3-focus-in-open-map.png.

**5. Nothing in the app tells me which keys work.**
- Place: whole app; facts.txt says there is "no help link inside the app" and no accessibility statement.
- What is there: '+' and the arrow keys move the map, Escape closes a panel, arrow keys reorder layers and move along the profile, but I only know that from the test record. Only the layers and profile hints are on screen.
- Why it matters: I rely on keys; I shouldn't have to guess them.
- Seriousness: slows it down.
- Change: a short "Keyboard" list in the app (reachable from the "Skip to map tools" area or the Versions panel), and a line on the map when it gets focus.

**6. Adding my own data was shown only as a drag onto the map.**
- Place: 14-csv-dropped.png ("parks.csv ... dragged onto the map"); 13-tool-add.png has a "Your own data" tab.
- What is there: the walk-through dragged the file. What "Your own data" offers (a "choose a file" button?) is not in the packet.
- Why it matters: I can't hold a button down and move the ball across the screen.
- Seriousness: stops that step if drag is the only way; needs checking in the live app.
- Change: make sure "Your own data" has an ordinary file button that works with Enter, and say so there.

**7. Sketch: drawing by keyboard is not shown.**
- Place: 20-tool-sketch.png: "Choose a drawing tool on the right of the map, or select a sketch to change it." with a "Go to the drawing tools" button; small icon strip left of the panel.
- What is there: the button to jump to the drawing tools is helpful. But drawing itself was not tried by keyboard, and the icons in the strip are small and have no words.
- Why it matters: drawing a line or shape normally means holding and dragging, or several exact clicks.
- Seriousness: stops the step if a lesson asks me to mark the map; needs checking in the live app.
- Change: same as finding 1: Enter to drop a point at the map centre, a "Finish" button, and word labels on the drawing tools.

**8. A lot of Tab presses to get anywhere.**
- Place: access.txt. From first open, the tools start at press 12 ("Basemaps"); in Open map, 22 presses go through the categories before any map card, and each card has three stops ("Share", "Open map", "Map's description") with 121 maps.
- What is there: "Skip to map tools" is the first stop, which helps, but where it lands was not recorded (needs checking). In Open map the Search box is press 3, which cuts the list down (05-open-search.png).
- Why it matters: every press is slow for me and I tire.
- Seriousness: slows it down.
- Change: in Open map and Add layer, a "Skip to the maps" link after Search; keep Search first.

**9. Small targets for the trackball.**
- Place: 09-layer-menu.png (row of eight small icons under each layer, and the six-dot drag handles); 21-tool-bookmarks.png (the small "✎"); the "×" on every panel; the arrows and "1 of 17" in the pop-up (09); access.txt marks "Esri" as 20x21 and the version button as 107x21.
- What is there: icon-only buttons about the size of a letter, close together, with the layer's delete (bin) next to "information".
- Why it matters: a slip on the layer row could delete a layer. I can't tell what the icons are without hovering (whether a name shows on keyboard focus needs checking).
- Seriousness: slows it down; the delete slip is the risk.
- Change: bigger hit areas with more space, the name shown on focus as well as hover, and an undo or "are you sure" on delete.

**10. Some places where focus goes can't be seen or go nowhere.**
- Place: access.txt: after Enter on a tool, focus is on the heading ("Open map", "Save") "[NO VISIBLE FOCUS RING]"; Tab press 24, after "Save", is "BODY (nothing focused)".
- Why it matters: for one press I don't know where I am.
- Seriousness: minor.
- Change: show a ring on the heading, or put focus on the first control; remove the empty stop.

**11. Getting out: proved for two places only.**
- Place: access.txt: Escape closed Save and focus went back to the "Save" button; the Open map window kept focus inside and has "Close" as its first stop.
- What is there: Escape on the Open map window, the pop-up, the Filter panel (10), the table (11), the Elevation dock and the "Save your map" box (25) was not recorded.
- Seriousness: needs checking in the live app; a stuck panel would stop the lesson.
- Change: Escape closes every panel, window and pop-up and puts focus back where I was.

**12. 3D by keyboard was not tried.**
- Place: 22-to-3d.png; the "3D" button is reachable (Tab press 21).
- What is there: no record of turning or tilting the 3D view with keys.
- Seriousness: needs checking in the live app; slows it down if a lesson uses one of the 3 3D maps.

## What works well for me
- Opening a map works by keyboard start to finish: Enter on "Open map", focus stays in the window, Enter on a card, and focus comes back to the tool (access.txt, k3).
- The map moves and zooms with keys ('+' and arrows), and Zoom in, Zoom out, Home and Compass are real buttons.
- Drag has a key alternative where it is named: "Drag the handle, or press the up and down arrow keys on it, to change the drawing order."; the profile chart says "The arrow keys move along it."; the filter has an "Enter a number" box beside the slider on the histogram.
- Nothing in the packet is timed, no sign-in is needed, and Save fills in the file name for me, so there is very little typing.

## One sentence
I could open a map, switch layers and save with the keyboard, so I would try it, but not for a lesson that needs Measure, Elevation or pop-ups; the single change that matters most is a keyboard way to place and finish points on the map (Enter at the map centre plus a "Finish" button) so nothing needs a double-click or an exact click.
