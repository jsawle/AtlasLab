# T3 Luis: shared devices and short periods

Review of School Atlas 5.0.0-beta.43, from the packet only (notes.txt, facts.txt, screenshots 01 to 27). All screenshots are a 1366 x 768 Chromebook-size screen. The packet has nothing showing the app on a phone, on a slow connection, or with any site blocked, so several findings below say "needs checking in the live app".

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. A student's saved map lives on one device, and my cart hands out a different device next time
- **Place:** 24-tool-save.png and 25-save-pressed.png, Save panel and "Save your map" window; facts.txt.
- **What is there:** Save says "Save your map as an .amap file, wherever you choose. No sign-in needed. After the first save, Save updates the same file." The other route is "Sign in to save maps to your ArcGIS Online account." facts.txt says the file is on the device, and "My map files" lists files "saved or opened before on this device and browser profile".
- **Why it matters to me:** I book the cart a week ahead and students rarely get the same Chromebook twice. Work saved to period 3's Chromebook on Tuesday is not on the one a student picks up next Tuesday. My students do not have ArcGIS accounts. Nothing on the Save panel tells a student this, or says where to put the file so it follows them (for example their school Google Drive). Whether the "wherever you choose" window on a Chromebook offers Google Drive, and whether a file reopened from there keeps saving back to it, needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson (any lesson that runs over two periods).
- **Suggested change:** One plain line on the Save panel, such as "On a shared computer, save to your own Drive or a USB stick so you can open it on another computer." Test and state in a teacher note what happens on a managed Chromebook.

### 2. Nothing shows what the class sees when a site is blocked or slow
- **Place:** notes.txt, "HOSTS contacted" line at the end; facts.txt "Web addresses it contacts"; no screenshot shows an error or a waiting message.
- **What is there:** The app needs the page itself (jsawle.github.io) plus about a dozen Esri addresses (js.arcgis.com, www.arcgis.com, services.arcgis.com, tiles.arcgis.com, basemaps.arcgis.com, cdn.arcgis.com, static.arcgis.com, elevation3d.arcgis.com, services.arcgisonline.com and others, "and map tile servers named by a map"). The packet does not show what appears on screen if any one of these is blocked by a district filter.
- **Why it matters to me:** My district filters the web. If one address is blocked I need the screen to say so in words I can read to the tech office, not a blank or half-drawn map with 30 students waiting. I also cannot tell whether github.io is allowed in my district; that needs checking with the district, and I only find out on the day unless I test on a student Chromebook first. Whether the app shows any message when something fails to load needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson.
- **Suggested change:** A clear on-screen message when the map, a layer or the map list cannot load ("This layer could not load. Your school network may be blocking tiles.arcgis.com"). Put the list of addresses on one printable page a teacher can send to the tech office ahead of time. facts.txt says there is "no teacher how-to guide and no help link inside the app", so today there is nowhere for that list to live.

### 3. No "still loading" sign; half-drawn maps look like the finished thing
- **Place:** 14-csv-dropped.png (map area, left and top: blurred grey blocks next to sharp imagery); 15-csv-added.png (after "Add to map" the four points cannot be seen, though the Map layers badge has gone from 5 to 6; they appear by 17-tool-profile.png); 22-to-3d.png (3D still soft and blurred 45 seconds after clicking, and no earthquake points or added points visible).
- **What is there:** In none of these is there a spinner, bar or word telling the user the map is still drawing. The packet notes the 3D slowness is partly the test machine (no graphics card).
- **Why it matters to me:** A school cart Chromebook on classroom wifi is closer to that test machine than to a new laptop. In a 45-minute period, 45 seconds of nothing per student is a real cost, and students will click again or call me over because they cannot tell "slow" from "broken". From the projector, I cannot tell the class "wait" with any confidence.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down; 3D could stop it on weak devices (needs checking on a real cart Chromebook).
- **Suggested change:** A visible "Loading..." sign while the map or a layer is drawing. For 3D, a short line before switching, such as "3D can be slow on older computers."

### 4. The Legend panel is empty
- **Place:** 12-tool-legend.png, Legend panel top right.
- **What is there:** The panel shows the word "Legend" and a close button, and nothing else. The map behind it has several layers switched on (yellow, red, cyan and grey dots, white lines).
- **Why it matters to me:** When I teach from the projector, the legend is what the class reads to fill in the paper sheet: "what do the red dots mean?" With an empty legend I have to open Map layers and hunt, with the class watching. It may simply not have finished loading when the picture was taken; that needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down (stops it if it is always empty for this map).
- **Suggested change:** Make sure the legend fills for every layer that is switched on; while it is loading, say "Loading legend..." so an empty box is never shown.

### 5. No warning seen before unsaved work is replaced
- **Place:** 27-open-another-unsaved.png, Open map window with "Open map" pressed on "Major Earthquakes (1950-2023)".
- **What is there:** The step is described as "With unsaved changes, another map chosen in Open map." The screenshot shows only the Open map window; no "you have unsaved changes" message is visible, and notes.txt lists no words from one.
- **Why it matters to me:** Students in pairs on a shared device click fast. If a pair opens another map five minutes before the bell and their sketch and added data vanish with no warning, that pair has nothing to hand in and I have no time to redo it. Whether a warning appears a moment after this picture was taken needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson for the students it hits.
- **Suggested change:** Before opening another map or a blank map with unsaved changes, ask: "Save your map first?" with Save, Don't save, Cancel. Check the same when the tab is closed.

### 6. "My map files" on a shared device: the next student sees the last student's list
- **Place:** 04-tool-open.png, "My map files" tab in Open map; 02-version-about.png, Versions row "Saved maps list": "Maps saved on this device, newest first, kept in this browser."
- **What is there:** The list of saved maps is kept per device and browser profile. The packet has no screenshot of the "My map files" tab itself, and nothing about clearing the list.
- **Why it matters to me:** If each student signs in to the Chromebook with their own school account, they should each have their own list. But when devices are short I have students on a shared or guest login, and phones get passed around. Then period 4 sees period 3's file names and may open and save over someone else's map. Whether one student can open and overwrite another's file from that list, and whether the list can be cleared, needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down; could lose work.
- **Suggested change:** A "Clear this list" button on "My map files", and a line saying whose device the list belongs to ("Maps saved on this computer").

### 7. Students on phones: the packet shows nothing, and the save wording points them to another browser
- **Place:** 25-save-pressed.png, "Save your map" window; all screenshots are 1366 x 768.
- **What is there:** "This browser saves files to its Downloads folder. To choose a folder, use Chrome or Edge." facts.txt says in Safari and Firefox "the file goes to the downloads folder each time". There is no screenshot at phone size. The bottom toolbar has ten buttons across (Basemaps to Save) and the side panels are about a quarter of a Chromebook screen wide.
- **Why it matters to me:** Some of my students will be on phones because the cart is short. I cannot tell from the packet whether the ten-button toolbar and the panels fit a phone, or whether a student can find a saved file again in a phone's downloads. "To choose a folder, use Chrome or Edge" is no help to a student on a phone who has one browser (and in this test it was shown inside Chrome). Needs checking in the live app on a real phone.
- **How serious:** Stops the lesson for those students if it does not fit; otherwise slows it down.
- **Suggested change:** Test on a small Android phone and an iPhone and state plainly what works there. Reword to something that does not send the student elsewhere: "Your map will be saved to this device's Downloads folder."

### 8. A "Guided Lesson" opens with no guidance on screen
- **Place:** 06-map-opened.png, title bar "Exploring earthquakes and their causes (Guided Lesson)" with a small round "i" button beside it.
- **What is there:** The map opens with dots and lines and no steps, question or task visible. The Open map card says "What causes earthquakes, and how do they affect people?" but that is gone once the map opens. What the "i" button shows is not in the packet (needs checking in the live app).
- **Why it matters to me:** Running from one projected screen, I need the task visible to the class, or printable for the paper sheet. With pairs on Chromebooks, a map with no instruction means hands up straight away. If the lesson steps are a separate document, the app does not link to it anywhere I can see.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down.
- **Suggested change:** If a map has lesson steps or a guiding question, show them in a panel when the map opens (closable), and include them in Print.

### 9. Small grey text and unlabelled icons are hard to read from the back of the room
- **Place:** 08-tool-layers.png, Map layers panel (rows of small icons under each layer with no words; layer names "Historic Major Earthquakes", "Other causes of earthquakes" and "Global Active Earthquake Faults" in pale grey); 18-profile-drawn.png, the small grey line of sources and the small labels "Length", "Highest", "Lowest"; 07-map-popup.png, the pop-up sits over the middle of the map.
- **What is there:** The bottom toolbar has clear words under each picture, which is good. Inside the panels, the tools for a layer are small pictures only, and helper text is small and grey.
- **Why it matters to me:** When I say "click the filter button" to a class following along, there is no word on screen to point at, and from a projector in a lit room pale grey text washes out. "Global Active Earthquake Faults" is ticked on but its name is still pale grey like the switched-off layers, so the class cannot tell on from off by the name.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down.
- **Suggested change:** Show a word beside or under each layer tool, or at least on hover and focus (needs checking whether hover names exist). Darker helper text. A simple "larger text" or presenter setting would help projector teaching most.

### 10. Paper: Print and Screenshot exist, but the packet does not show what comes out
- **Place:** 24-tool-save.png, Save panel tiles "Screenshot" ("Save a picture of the map as it is now.") and "Print" ("Print the map, or save it as a PDF.").
- **What is there:** The two tiles, but no picture of a printed page.
- **Why it matters to me:** My fallback when the cart falls through is the class working from paper. A printed map is only useful if it carries the title, the legend and the scale bar, and reads in black and white on the school copier. The opened lesson map sits on a dark satellite background (06-map-opened.png), which copies as a black page. None of this can be judged from the packet; needs checking in the live app.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down (stops the paper version if the legend is missing).
- **Suggested change:** Make Print include title, legend, scale and source line, and offer a "light background for printing" choice.

### 11. Getting a class to the right map without a link
- **Place:** 04-tool-open.png and 05-open-search.png, Open map window; "Share" on each card.
- **What is there:** To reach the lesson map a student opens the app, clicks Open map, types "earthquake" in Search, and picks from 11 results where four start with similar names ("Real-Time Earthquakes" and "Real-Time Earthquakes with chart" sit next to each other). Each card has "Share". What Share gives (a long link, a short one, a code to scan) is not shown in the packet; needs checking in the live app. facts.txt: "It cannot be opened with a class roster and has no link to Google Classroom".
- **Why it matters to me:** With no learning platform link, I write the address on the board. "jsawle.github.io/AtlasLab" then "Open map, search earthquake, first one" works but costs a few minutes of a 45-minute period and some students will open the wrong one. Phone users cannot be expected to type a long link.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down.
- **Suggested change:** Have Share offer a short link and a scannable code I can put on the projector or the paper sheet. Share also seems to be only for the ready-made maps; a map a student has changed can only go as a file.

### 12. It is a test version with test content, and there is no help
- **Place:** 01-first-open.png, bottom right "v5.0.0-beta.43"; facts.txt.
- **What is there:** facts.txt: "a test version, not yet an official product"; "The maps in 'Open map' come from a test content group"; "no teacher how-to guide and no help link inside the app". Also in the walk, the Measure tool's Distance button could not be clicked by the test ("measure-distance FAILED", 16-tool-measure.png); whether that is a fault in the app or in the test needs checking in the live app.
- **Why it matters to me:** I plan a week ahead around a cart booking. If a map is renamed or removed from a test group between my planning and my lesson, I find out in front of the class. With no help link, I have nowhere to look when something goes wrong mid-period.
- **How serious:** Slows the lesson down; a risk rather than a seen fault.
- **Suggested change:** A Help button with a one-page teacher start sheet (address list for the tech office, how saving works on shared devices, what works on phones). Say on screen that this is a test version so teachers know what to expect.

## What works well for me
- No account and no install: the first screen (01-first-open.png) is a working map, and the sign-in window says "You don't need an account to use the app or to save maps on this device."
- The bottom toolbar has big buttons with a plain word under each (Basemaps, Open map, Map layers, Legend, Measure, Save), easy to call out from the front of the room.
- The elevation profile (18-profile-drawn.png) puts a sentence in plain words under the chart, "The line is 120 mi long. Its highest point is 10,607 ft and its lowest 3,950 ft.", which the class can copy straight onto paper.
- Open map search and categories (05-open-search.png) found the lesson map in one word, and Screenshot and Print sit right beside Save.

## Would I use it?
I would use it from my own projected screen as it stands, but I would not yet hand it to a class on the shared cart and phones; the single change that would matter most is making saved work safe and findable on shared devices (say on the Save panel where to put the file so it follows the student, and warn before unsaved work is replaced).
